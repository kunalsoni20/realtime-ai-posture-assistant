Built with Precision Computer Vision & AI

A full-stack fitness intelligence pipeline integrating pose estimation, vector geometry, LLMs, and SQLite analytics.

📐

Vector Trigonometry

Calculates 2D joint angles dynamically with NumPy arctan2 across 33 MediaPipe pose landmarks, filtering out occlusion and noise.

⚡

25+ FPS HUD Overlay

High-performance OpenCV telemetry rendering showing live angles, rep counters, stage transitions (UP/DOWN), and posture warnings.

🎙️

CoachLLM & Audio TTS

In-memory text-to-speech synthesis (gTTS) providing immediate auditory form cues (e.g., "Keep chest up!", "Hit 90 degrees!").

📊

SQLite Session Analytics

Automatically records workout repetitions, accuracy scores, durations, and calories burned for persistent lifetime tracking.

🚀

Cloud Simulation Fallback

Synthetic workout generator allowing complete testing on cloud platforms (Streamlit Cloud, Render) without physical webcam limits.

🛡️

Debounced Rep Logic

Smart state machines preventing ghost repetition spikes with 1.2s debouncing and deepest joint angle verification.

BIOMECHANICAL STANDARDS

Supported Exercises & Angle Thresholds

🦵

Squats

  • Tracked Joints: Hip → Knee → Ankle
  • Standing Angle: > 160.0°
  • Parallel Depth: < 95.0°
  • Flaw Detection: Shallow depth, chest collapse, knees forward
🤸

Push-ups

  • Tracked Joints: Shoulder → Elbow → Wrist & Plank
  • Plank Extended: > 155.0°
  • Bottom Flexed: < 90.0°
  • Flaw Detection: Sagging hips (<155°), high hips (>195°), partial ROM
💪

Bicep Curls

  • Tracked Joints: Shoulder → Elbow → Wrist
  • Arm Extended: > 155.0°
  • Arm Curled: < 45.0°
  • Flaw Detection: Elbow swinging flare, upper torso cheat momentum
